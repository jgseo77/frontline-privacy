FRONTLINE Privacy Policy

FRONTLINE: Puzzle War — a block-puzzle strategy game where you push the front line
Effective: September 30, 2026 · Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Developer: HYPERSPEED

This policy explains how FRONTLINE ("the App"), provided by HYPERSPEED ("the Developer"), handles data. The campaign and practice modes work without signing in, and their records stay on your device. A server account is created only if you sign in to play Versus against other players (section 1).

1. Information the Developer Processes

Without signing in

Campaign progress, stars, cosmetics, sound settings and practice records are stored only on your device and are not sent to the Developer.

Versus (optional · Sign in with Apple)

If you Sign in with Apple to play Versus, an account is created on the Developer's server (Supabase) with the following:
ItemDetailsVisible to other players?
Account identifierA random ID used for sign-inNo
Email addressThe address Apple provides. If you choose "Hide My Email", Apple provides a relay address insteadNo
Display nameA name you choose or that Apple providesYes — on the opponent card
Match recordsMatch replays (the sequence of your block moves and troop choices), win/loss record, main troop distribution, skill rating, time of last matchYes — replays are played back as opponents for other players, and your record and main troops appear on the opponent card
  • Purpose — to find opponents of similar skill.
  • The Developer does not view or use this data except for operations and troubleshooting, and never sells or shares it with third parties.
  • Your Apple ID password and payment details are never shared with the Developer.

2. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To improve the App we may process: usage events (stage start and result, Versus results, cosmetics equipped, etc.), an app-instance identifier, device and OS info, approximate location from your IP address (country), and app errors. This is used only for statistics and for tuning difficulty and features. It is not linked to your Versus account and never used for tracking across other companies' apps or websites · Firebase Privacy

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch (stage boosts) and interstitial ads shown between matches every few matches. Google and its partners may collect: advertising identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA), approximate location from your IP address (country/region), and device and ad-interaction data. This is processed by Google, not the Developer · Google Privacy Policy · AdMob data use

iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS a system prompt may ask whether to allow tracking. The App works normally if you decline; you will see non-personalized ads.

Consent for EEA, UK and Switzerland users

In these regions a Google-certified consent tool (UMP) asks for your consent to ad and analytics processing. If you decline, ads are still shown but non-personalized, and analytics collection does not start until your choice is known. You can change or withdraw consent anytime from [개인정보 옵션] (Privacy options) on the Home screen (shown in those regions). Where applicable U.S. state laws apply, you may be offered an opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

In-app purchases

The App currently offers no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Delete in the App (immediate) — tap "계정 삭제" (Delete account) under your name on the Home screen to remove your server account and match records at once.
  • Request without the App — if you have already uninstalled the App, follow the account and data deletion guide to request by email. We will delete and reply within 30 days of receiving the request.
  • On-device data — deleted when you uninstall the App. On Android, if Auto Backup is enabled, it may be backed up to your own Google account and restored on reinstall (Settings > Google > Backup). The Developer cannot access these backups.
  • Server backups — copies in our provider's (Supabase) backups expire automatically after the backup retention period.
  • Ads — reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings.

You may request access to, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing of your data at any time. You can change your display name in the App; send any other request to the contact below. Users in the EEA, the UK and other regions with applicable laws may exercise the rights those laws provide in the same way.
